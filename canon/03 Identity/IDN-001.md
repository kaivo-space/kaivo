# 1. Purpose

The Identity Vision defines the canonical vision for Identity within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the long-term conceptual direction for Identity and provides the architectural foundation for all Identity-related artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Vision defines:

* The purpose of Identity.
* Identity objectives.
* Identity principles.
* Identity boundaries.
* Conformance expectations.

The Identity Vision does not define:

* Identity implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Authentication.
* Authorization.
* Storage mechanisms.
* User interface behavior.

---

# 3. Vision

Identity is the canonical representation of uniquely identifiable entities within Kaivo Core.

The Identity domain provides a consistent conceptual foundation that enables identification, organization, traceability, and reuse while preserving the integrity and independence of every Identity instance.

---

# 4. Identity Objectives

The Identity domain aims to:

* Preserve unique identity.
* Maintain canonical consistency.
* Enable reusable identity structures.
* Support traceable relationships.
* Preserve semantic integrity.

---

# 5. Identity Principles

Identity shall be:

* Canonical.
* Unique.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Identity Relationships

The Identity Vision establishes the conceptual direction for Identity artifacts.

Identity shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Kit.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve the identity of every participating entity.

Identity shall not redefine concepts established by governing Core artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Identity Vision does not define:

* Authentication mechanisms.
* Authorization models.
* Security policies.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Identity shall possess exactly one canonical identity.

---

### C2

Identity shall preserve canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Identity shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Identity shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

Identity shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

Identity shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Identity artifact conforms to this vision if it:

* Preserves unique identity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model and Identity Specification.
