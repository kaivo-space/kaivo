# 1. Purpose

The Brand Architecture defines the canonical organizational structure of the Kaivo brand within the Identity domain.

It establishes the conceptual relationships between the Kaivo brand and its Identity artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Brand Architecture defines:

* Brand organization.
* Brand responsibilities.
* Brand relationships.
* Brand boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Brand Architecture does not define:

* Marketing strategy.
* Visual design.
* Product positioning.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Brand Principles

The Brand Architecture shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Cohesive.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Brand Organization

The Kaivo brand is represented as a canonical identity that provides a consistent reference for all related Identity artifacts.

Brand organization shall:

* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain clear responsibility boundaries.
* Preserve approved relationships.
* Support long-term consistency across the Identity domain.

---

# 5. Brand Relationships

The Brand Architecture shall:

* Conform to the Identity Vision.
* Conform to the Identity Principles.
* Conform to the Identity Architecture.
* Preserve the Core Values.
* Preserve the identity of every participating artifact.

The Brand Architecture shall not redefine responsibilities established by other canonical artifacts.

---

# 6. Brand Responsibilities

The Brand Architecture shall:

* Provide a coherent organizational structure for the Kaivo brand.
* Preserve canonical identity.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support traceability across the Identity domain.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Brand Architecture does not define:

* Brand identity guidelines.
* Logos.
* Typography.
* Color systems.
* Marketing assets.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

The Brand Architecture shall preserve exactly one canonical brand identity.

---

### C2

Brand relationships shall preserve approved responsibility boundaries.

---

### C3

Brand organization shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Brand relationships shall maintain semantic consistency.

---

### C5

The Brand Architecture shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Brand Architecture shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Brand Architecture shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A Brand Architecture conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves canonical brand identity.
* Maintains approved architectural relationships.
* Preserves semantic consistency.
* Supports traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Vision, Identity Principles, Identity Architecture, and Core Values.
