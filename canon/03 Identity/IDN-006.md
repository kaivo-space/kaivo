# 1. Purpose

The Brand Governance defines the canonical governance principles for the Kaivo brand within the Identity domain.

It establishes the governance responsibilities and constraints required to preserve the integrity, consistency, and long-term stability of the canonical brand while remaining implementation-neutral.

---

# 2. Scope

The Brand Governance defines:

* Brand governance principles.
* Brand governance responsibilities.
* Brand governance boundaries.
* Brand conformance requirements.

The Brand Governance does not define:

* Marketing strategy.
* Visual identity implementation.
* Product positioning.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Brand Governance shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Reusable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Governance Responsibilities

Brand Governance shall:

* Preserve the canonical brand identity.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Preserve approved architectural relationships.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Identity artifacts.

Brand Governance shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 5. Governance Relationships

Brand Governance shall:

* Conform to the Identity Vision.
* Conform to the Identity Principles.
* Conform to the Identity Architecture.
* Preserve the Core Values.
* Preserve the Brand Architecture.

Governance relationships shall preserve the integrity of participating artifacts.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The Brand Governance does not define:

* Logos.
* Typography.
* Color systems.
* Marketing assets.
* Repository implementation.
* Runtime execution.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Brand Governance shall preserve exactly one canonical brand identity.

---

### C2

Brand Governance shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Brand Governance shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Brand Governance shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

Brand Governance shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Brand Governance shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Brand Governance shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

A Brand Governance implementation conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves the canonical brand identity.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains approved architectural relationships.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Vision, Identity Principles, Identity Architecture, Core Values, and Brand Architecture.
