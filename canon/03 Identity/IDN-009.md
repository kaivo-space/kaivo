# 1. Purpose

The Identity Lifecycle defines the canonical lifecycle of Identity within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual lifecycle through which an Identity is defined, maintained, governed, and preserved while ensuring consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Lifecycle defines:

* Identity lifecycle principles.
* Lifecycle stages.
* Lifecycle responsibilities.
* Lifecycle boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Identity Lifecycle does not define:

* Runtime behavior.
* Authentication.
* Authorization.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure.

---

# 3. Lifecycle Principles

The Identity Lifecycle shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Governed.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Lifecycle Stages

Every canonical Identity progresses through the following conceptual lifecycle:

## Definition

The Identity is conceptually defined according to the Identity Model and Identity Specification.

---

## Establishment

The Identity becomes a recognized canonical Identity within the approved Identity Architecture.

---

## Maintenance

The Identity is maintained while preserving canonical terminology, semantic integrity, and approved relationships.

---

## Evolution

The Identity may evolve through the approved governance process without redefining its canonical identity.

---

## Preservation

The Identity remains traceable and authoritative throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 5. Lifecycle Responsibilities

The Identity Lifecycle shall:

* Preserve canonical identity.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Preserve approved terminology.
* Maintain traceability.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Identity artifacts.

The Identity Lifecycle shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Lifecycle Boundaries

The Identity Lifecycle does not define:

* Security controls.
* Repository implementation.
* Deployment procedures.
* Runtime execution.
* Application-specific behavior.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Identity shall follow one canonical lifecycle.

---

### C2

Identity shall preserve exactly one canonical identity throughout its lifecycle.

---

### C3

Lifecycle evolution shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Lifecycle governance shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Identity Lifecycle shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Identity Lifecycle shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Identity Lifecycle shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

An Identity implementation conforms to this lifecycle if it:

* Preserves one canonical identity.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Vision, Identity Principles, Identity Architecture, Core Values, Brand Architecture, Brand Governance, Communication Framework, and Visual Identity System.
