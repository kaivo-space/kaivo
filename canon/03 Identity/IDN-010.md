# 1. Purpose

The Identity Index provides the canonical index of Identity artifacts within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a single authoritative reference for locating, organizing, and understanding all canonical Identity artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and architectural integrity.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Index defines:

* The canonical Identity artifact catalog.
* Identity artifact organization.
* Identity reference relationships.
* Identity index responsibilities.

The Identity Index does not define:

* Identity semantics.
* Architectural principles.
* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Repository implementation.

# 4. Responsibilities

The Identity Index shall:

* Provide an authoritative catalog of Identity artifacts.
* Preserve canonical identifiers.
* Preserve canonical artifact names.
* Maintain traceability across the Identity domain.
* Support discovery of approved Identity artifacts.

The Identity Index shall not redefine or replace the artifacts it references.

---

# 5. Organization Principles

The Identity Index shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Reusable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Identity Index does not:

* Replace normative Identity artifacts.
* Introduce new Identity concepts.
* Redefine canonical terminology.
* Modify approved Identity artifacts.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Identity artifact shall appear at most once in the Identity Index.

---

### C2

The Identity Index shall preserve canonical identifiers.

---

### C3

The Identity Index shall preserve canonical artifact names.

---

### C4

The Identity Index shall remain consistent with approved Identity artifacts.

---

### C5

The Identity Index shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Identity Index shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

An implementation conforms to the Identity Index if it:

* References approved Identity artifacts accurately.
* Preserves canonical identifiers.
* Maintains consistency with approved Identity artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
