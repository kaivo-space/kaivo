# 1. Purpose

The Identity Principles define the canonical principles governing Identity within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve the integrity, consistency, and long-term stability of Identity across all Identity-related artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Principles define:

* Identity principles.
* Identity consistency requirements.
* Identity conformance expectations.
* Identity boundaries.

The Identity Principles do not define:

* Authentication.
* Authorization.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure.

---

# 3. Identity Principles

## Principle 1 — Uniqueness

Every Identity shall represent exactly one uniquely identifiable entity.

---

## Principle 2 — Canonical Consistency

Identity artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 3 — Stability

A canonical Identity shall remain stable throughout its lifecycle unless modified through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 4 — Traceability

Identity shall remain traceable across all related canonical artifacts.

---

## Principle 5 — Reusability

Identity definitions shall be reusable without redefining approved concepts.

---

## Principle 6 — Semantic Integrity

Identity shall preserve its approved meaning and relationships.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Identity shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Identity Requirements

Every Identity artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved Identity artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Identity Principles do not define:

* Security policies.
* Authentication mechanisms.
* Authorization models.
* Runtime execution.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Identity shall possess exactly one canonical identity.

---

### C2

Identity shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Identity shall maintain semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Identity artifacts shall preserve traceability.

---

### C5

Identity artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Identity artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Identity artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Conformance

An Identity artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves unique identity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Vision.
