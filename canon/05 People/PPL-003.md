# 1. Purpose

The Organizational Structure defines the canonical organizational structure of the People domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual organization of People, Roles, and their relationships while preserving canonical consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Organizational Structure defines:

* Organizational structure principles.
* Organizational responsibilities.
* Organizational relationships.
* Organizational boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Organizational Structure does not define:

* Human resource policies.
* Employment contracts.
* Operational procedures.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Organizational Principles

The Organizational Structure shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Hierarchical.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Organizational Structure

The People domain organizes canonical People through defined organizational relationships.

The organizational structure shall:

* Preserve the identity of every Person.
* Preserve approved Roles.
* Preserve approved relationships.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support long-term organizational stability.

Every organizational element shall have one defined responsibility within the canonical structure.

---

# 5. Organizational Relationships

The Organizational Structure shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Organizational Structure shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Organizational Responsibilities

The Organizational Structure shall:

* Preserve canonical identities.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain semantic integrity.
* Maintain organizational traceability.
* Support reusable organizational structures.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Organizational Structure does not define:

* Organizational governance.
* Compensation models.
* Security policies.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Person shall belong to the canonical organizational structure.

---

### C2

Every organizational relationship shall preserve canonical identity.

---

### C3

Organizational responsibilities shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Organizational relationships shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Organizational Structure shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Organizational Structure shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Organizational Structure shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An organizational structure conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves canonical identities.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains organizational traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model, Identity Specification, People Vision, and People Principles.
