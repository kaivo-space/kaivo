# 1. Purpose

The People Vision defines the canonical vision for the People domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the long-term conceptual direction for representing people as canonical entities while preserving consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The People Vision defines:

* The purpose of the People domain.
* People objectives.
* People principles.
* Domain boundaries.
* Conformance expectations.

The People Vision does not define:

* Human resource processes.
* Organizational structures.
* Authentication.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Vision

The People domain provides the canonical representation of persons within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a consistent conceptual foundation for identifying, describing, organizing, and relating people while preserving their identity, responsibilities, and relationships throughout the Kaivo Canon.

---

# 4. People Objectives

The People domain aims to:

* Preserve unique person identities.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support reusable person representations.
* Preserve traceable relationships.
* Enable long-term maintainability.

---

# 5. People Principles

People shall be:

* Canonical.
* Unique.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. People Relationships

The People Vision establishes the conceptual direction for People artifacts.

People artifacts shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.

People artifacts shall not redefine concepts established by governing Core artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The People Vision does not define:

* Employment policies.
* Organizational hierarchy.
* Security mechanisms.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Person shall possess exactly one canonical identity.

---

### C2

People artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

People artifacts shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

People artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

People artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

People artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A People artifact conforms to this vision if it:

* Preserves unique identity.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model and Identity Specification.
