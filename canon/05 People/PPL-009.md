# 1. Purpose

The Workforce Model defines the canonical conceptual model of the workforce within the People domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the principles, structure, and relationships governing the representation of the workforce while preserving canonical consistency, semantic integrity, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Workforce Model defines:

* Workforce principles.
* Workforce structure.
* Workforce relationships.
* Workforce responsibilities.
* Conformance requirements.

The Workforce Model does not define:

* Employment contracts.
* Compensation models.
* Human resource operations.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Workforce Principles

The Workforce Model shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Scalable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Workforce Organization

The workforce is represented as a canonical collection of People fulfilling defined Roles within the approved Organizational Structure.

The Workforce Model shall:

* Preserve the identity of every Person.
* Preserve approved Roles.
* Preserve approved organizational relationships.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support reusable workforce structures.

---

# 5. Workforce Relationships

The Workforce Model shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Conform to the Organizational Structure.
* Conform to the Roles & Competency Framework.
* Conform to the Leadership Framework.
* Conform to the Collaboration Framework.
* Conform to the Performance Framework.
* Conform to the People Governance.

The Workforce Model shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Workforce Responsibilities

The Workforce Model shall:

* Preserve canonical identities.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain semantic integrity.
* Maintain workforce traceability.
* Preserve compatibility with governing People artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Workforce Model does not define:

* Recruitment processes.
* Performance evaluation methods.
* Organizational policy.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every workforce participant shall possess exactly one canonical identity.

---

### C2

The Workforce Model shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

The Workforce Model shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

The Workforce Model shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Workforce Model shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Workforce Model shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Workforce Model shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A workforce implementation conforms to this model if it:

* Preserves canonical identities.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains workforce traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model, Identity Specification, People Vision, People Principles, Organizational Structure, Roles & Competency Framework, Leadership Framework, Collaboration Framework, Performance Framework, and People Governance.
