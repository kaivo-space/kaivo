# 1. Purpose

The People Index provides the canonical index of People artifacts within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a single authoritative reference for locating, organizing, and understanding all canonical People artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and architectural integrity.

---

# 2. Scope

The People Index defines:

* The canonical People artifact catalog.
* People artifact organization.
* People reference relationships.
* People index responsibilities.

The People Index does not define:

* People semantics.
* Organizational policies.
* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Repository implementation.

# 4. Responsibilities

The People Index shall:

* Provide an authoritative catalog of People artifacts.
* Preserve canonical identifiers.
* Preserve canonical artifact names.
* Maintain traceability across the People domain.
* Support discovery of approved People artifacts.

The People Index shall not redefine or replace the artifacts it references.

---

# 5. Organization Principles

The People Index shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Reusable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The People Index does not:

* Replace normative People artifacts.
* Introduce new People concepts.
* Redefine canonical terminology.
* Modify approved People artifacts.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every People artifact shall appear at most once in the People Index.

---

### C2

The People Index shall preserve canonical identifiers.

---

### C3

The People Index shall preserve canonical artifact names.

---

### C4

The People Index shall remain consistent with approved People artifacts.

---

### C5

The People Index shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The People Index shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

An implementation conforms to the People Index if it:

* References approved People artifacts accurately.
* Preserves canonical identifiers.
* Maintains consistency with approved People artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
