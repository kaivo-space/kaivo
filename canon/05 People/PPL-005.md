# 1. Purpose

The Leadership Framework defines the canonical framework governing leadership within the People domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize leadership while preserving canonical consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Leadership Framework defines:

* Leadership principles.
* Leadership responsibilities.
* Leadership relationships.
* Leadership boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Leadership Framework does not define:

* Management methodologies.
* Performance evaluation.
* Human resource policies.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Leadership Principles

Leadership shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Responsible.
* Stable.
* Reusable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Leadership Responsibilities

Leadership shall:

* Provide direction within approved organizational responsibilities.
* Preserve canonical identity.
* Preserve approved terminology.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support traceable organizational relationships.

Leadership shall not redefine approved canonical concepts or responsibilities.

---

# 5. Leadership Relationships

The Leadership Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Conform to the Organizational Structure.
* Conform to the Roles & Competency Framework.

Leadership relationships shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

# 6. Leadership Responsibilities within the Framework

The Leadership Framework shall:

* Preserve canonical leadership responsibilities.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain organizational traceability.
* Support reusable leadership structures.
* Maintain compatibility with governing People artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Leadership Framework does not define:

* Organizational governance policies.
* Employment contracts.
* Compensation structures.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every leadership responsibility shall have one canonical definition.

---

### C2

Leadership shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Leadership shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Leadership shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Leadership Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Leadership Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Leadership Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A leadership implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves canonical leadership responsibilities.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains organizational traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model, Identity Specification, People Vision, People Principles, Organizational Structure, and Roles & Competency Framework.
