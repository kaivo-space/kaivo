# 1. Purpose

The People Principles define the canonical principles governing the People domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve the integrity, consistency, traceability, and long-term stability of People-related artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The People Principles define:

* People principles.
* People consistency requirements.
* People conformance expectations.
* People boundaries.

The People Principles do not define:

* Human resource policies.
* Organizational structures.
* Authentication.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. People Principles

## Principle 1 — Unique Identity

Every Person shall represent exactly one uniquely identifiable entity.

---

## Principle 2 — Canonical Consistency

People artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 3 — Semantic Integrity

People artifacts shall preserve the approved meaning and relationships of every Person.

---

## Principle 4 — Traceability

People shall remain traceable across all related canonical artifacts.

---

## Principle 5 — Reusability

People structures shall support reuse without redefining approved canonical concepts.

---

## Principle 6 — Stability

Approved People concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

People shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. People Requirements

Every People artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved People artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The People Principles do not define:

* Organizational governance.
* Employment policies.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Person shall possess exactly one canonical identity.

---

### C2

People artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

People artifacts shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

People artifacts shall maintain traceability.

---

### C5

People artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

People artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

People artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Conformance

A People artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves unique identity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the People Vision.
