# 1. Purpose

The Roles & Competency Framework defines the canonical framework governing Roles and Competencies within the People domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize Roles and Competencies while preserving canonical consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Roles & Competency Framework defines:

* Role principles.
* Competency principles.
* Responsibility relationships.
* Framework boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Roles & Competency Framework does not define:

* Human resource policies.
* Recruitment processes.
* Performance evaluation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Framework Principles

The Roles & Competency Framework shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Roles

A Role represents a canonical responsibility within the People domain.

Every Role shall:

* Have a clearly defined responsibility.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support traceable organizational relationships.

---

# 5. Competencies

A Competency represents a canonical capability associated with one or more Roles.

Every Competency shall:

* Have a clearly defined meaning.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Remain reusable across Roles.
* Maintain compatibility with approved canonical artifacts.

---

# 6. Relationships

The Roles & Competency Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Conform to the Organizational Structure.

The framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 7. Responsibilities

The Roles & Competency Framework shall:

* Preserve canonical identities.
* Preserve approved terminology.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Maintain traceability across Roles and Competencies.
* Support reusable organizational structures.

---

# 8. Boundaries

The Roles & Competency Framework does not define:

* Employment contracts.
* Compensation models.
* Organizational governance.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 9. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Role shall possess one defined canonical responsibility.

---

### C2

Every Competency shall possess one canonical definition.

---

### C3

Roles and Competencies shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Roles and Competencies shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Roles & Competency Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Roles & Competency Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Roles & Competency Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 10. Conformance

A Roles & Competency implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves canonical Roles.
* Preserves canonical Competencies.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model, Identity Specification, People Vision, People Principles, and Organizational Structure.
