# 1. Purpose

The People Governance defines the canonical governance framework for the People domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the governance principles, responsibilities, and constraints required to preserve the integrity, consistency, traceability, and long-term stability of People artifacts while remaining implementation-neutral.

---

# 2. Scope

The People Governance defines:

* Governance principles.
* Governance responsibilities.
* Governance relationships.
* Governance boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The People Governance does not define:

* Human resource operations.
* Employment policies.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

People Governance shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Verifiable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Governance Responsibilities

People Governance shall:

* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve approved architectural relationships.
* Maintain People traceability.
* Maintain compatibility with governing People artifacts.

People Governance shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 5. Governance Relationships

The People Governance shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Conform to the Organizational Structure.
* Conform to the Roles & Competency Framework.
* Conform to the Leadership Framework.
* Conform to the Collaboration Framework.
* Conform to the Performance Framework.

Governance relationships shall preserve the integrity of participating artifacts.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The People Governance does not define:

* Organizational policies.
* Compensation systems.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

A People Governance implementation conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains People traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
