# 1. Purpose

The Collaboration Framework defines the canonical framework governing collaboration within the People domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to support effective collaboration while preserving canonical consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Collaboration Framework defines:

* Collaboration principles.
* Collaboration responsibilities.
* Collaboration relationships.
* Collaboration boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Collaboration Framework does not define:

* Communication platforms.
* Project management methodologies.
* Human resource policies.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Collaboration Principles

Collaboration shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Cooperative.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Collaboration Responsibilities

Collaboration shall:

* Preserve canonical identities.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Support traceable relationships between People and Roles.
* Maintain compatibility with governing People artifacts.

Collaboration shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 5. Collaboration Relationships

The Collaboration Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the People Vision.
* Conform to the People Principles.
* Conform to the Organizational Structure.
* Conform to the Roles & Competency Framework.
* Conform to the Leadership Framework.

Collaboration relationships shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

# 6. Collaboration Responsibilities within the Framework

The Collaboration Framework shall:

* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain collaboration traceability.
* Support reusable collaboration structures.
* Preserve approved architectural relationships.
* Maintain consistency across the People domain.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Collaboration Framework does not define:

* Organizational governance.
* Employment agreements.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

---

# 9. Conformance

A collaboration implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves canonical identities.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains collaboration traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model, Identity Specification, People Vision, People Principles, Organizational Structure, Roles & Competency Framework, and Leadership Framework.
