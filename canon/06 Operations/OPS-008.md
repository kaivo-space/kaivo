# 1. Purpose

The Operational Governance defines the canonical governance framework for the Operations domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the governance principles, responsibilities, and constraints required to preserve consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and long-term stability across all Operations artifacts while remaining implementation-neutral.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operational Governance defines:

* Governance principles.
* Governance responsibilities.
* Governance relationships.
* Governance boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Operational Governance does not define:

* Operational procedures.
* Organizational governance.
* Runtime execution.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Operational Governance shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Verifiable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Governance Responsibilities

Operational Governance shall:

* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve approved architectural relationships.
* Maintain operational traceability.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Operations artifacts.

Operational Governance shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 5. Governance Relationships

The Operational Governance shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Operations Vision.
* Conform to the Operations Principles.
* Conform to the Operations Architecture.
* Conform to the Process Framework.
* Conform to the Workflow Framework.
* Conform to the Service Management Framework.
* Conform to the Quality Management Framework.

Governance relationships shall preserve the integrity of participating artifacts.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The Operational Governance does not define:

* Organizational policies.
* Automation technologies.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

An Operational Governance implementation conforms to this document if it:
