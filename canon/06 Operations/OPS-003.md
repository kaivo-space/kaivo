# 1. Purpose

The Operations Architecture defines the canonical architectural organization of the Operations domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual structure, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize operational capabilities while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, consistency, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operations Architecture defines:

* Operational architectural organization.
* Operational responsibilities.
* Operational relationships.
* Operational boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Operations Architecture does not define:

* Business process implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Technical implementation.
* Operational tooling.

---

# 3. Architectural Principles

The Operations Architecture shall be:

* Canonical.
* Modular.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Architectural Organization

The Operations domain is organized as a coherent collection of canonical operational capabilities and supporting artifacts.

Each Operations artifact fulfills one distinct architectural responsibility while contributing to a unified operational architecture.

The Operations Architecture preserves these responsibilities without overlap or duplication.

---

# 5. Architectural Relationships

Operations artifacts shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Operations Vision.
* Conform to the Operations Principles.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

Operations artifacts shall not redefine responsibilities established by other canonical artifacts.

---

# 6. Architectural Responsibilities

The Operations Architecture shall:

* Organize operational capabilities into coherent architectural structures.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Preserve traceability across the Operations domain.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Operations Architecture does not define:

* Workflow engines.
* Operational automation.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Operations artifact shall have exactly one architectural responsibility.

---

### C2

Operational responsibilities shall not overlap.

---

### C3

Operations artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Operations artifacts shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C5

The Operations Architecture shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Operations Architecture shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Operations Architecture shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Operations artifact conforms to the Operations Architecture if it:

* Preserves its defined architectural responsibility.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Preserves approved dependency relationships.
* Preserves canonical terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Operations Vision and Operations Principles.
