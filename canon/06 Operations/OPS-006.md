# 1. Purpose

The Service Management Framework defines the canonical framework governing service management within the Operations domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize and manage services while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, consistency, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Service Management Framework defines:

* Service management principles.
* Service responsibilities.
* Service relationships.
* Service management boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Service Management Framework does not define:

* Service implementation.
* Operational tooling.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Service Management Principles

Service Management shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reliable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Service Organization

Services represent canonical operational capabilities delivered through approved operational structures.

Every service shall:

* Possess a clearly defined operational purpose.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain traceable relationships.
* Support operational reuse.
* Maintain architectural consistency.

---

# 5. Service Relationships

The Service Management Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Operations Vision.
* Conform to the Operations Principles.
* Conform to the Operations Architecture.
* Conform to the Process Framework.
* Conform to the Workflow Framework.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Service Management Framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Service Responsibilities

The Service Management Framework shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain service traceability.
* Support reusable service structures.
* Maintain architectural consistency across operational services.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Service Management Framework does not define:

* Service desk technologies.
* Automation platforms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every service shall possess one canonical operational purpose.

---

### C2

Services shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Services shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Services shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Service Management Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Service Management Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Service Management Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A service management implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves canonical service purpose.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains service traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Operations Vision, Operations Principles, Operations Architecture, Process Framework, and Workflow Framework.
