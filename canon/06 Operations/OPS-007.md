# 1. Purpose

The Quality Management Framework defines the canonical framework governing quality management within the Operations domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to ensure operational quality while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, consistency, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Quality Management Framework defines:

* Quality management principles.
* Quality responsibilities.
* Quality relationships.
* Quality management boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Quality Management Framework does not define:

* Quality assurance methodologies.
* Testing procedures.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Quality Management Principles

Quality Management shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Measurable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Quality Organization

Quality represents the canonical capability for maintaining the integrity and consistency of operational activities and services.

Quality Management shall:

* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Support operational reliability.
* Maintain traceable quality relationships.
* Preserve architectural consistency.
* Support continuous operational integrity.

---

# 5. Quality Relationships

The Quality Management Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Operations Vision.
* Conform to the Operations Principles.
* Conform to the Operations Architecture.
* Conform to the Process Framework.
* Conform to the Workflow Framework.
* Conform to the Service Management Framework.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Quality Management Framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Quality Responsibilities

The Quality Management Framework shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain quality traceability.
* Support reusable quality structures.
* Maintain architectural consistency across operational activities.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Quality Management Framework does not define:

* Testing tools.
* Monitoring platforms.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Quality Management shall preserve one canonical operational purpose.

---

### C2

Quality Management shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

---

### C4

Quality Management shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Quality Management Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Quality Management Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Quality Management Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A quality management implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves canonical quality purpose.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains quality traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Operations Vision, Operations Principles, Operations Architecture, Process Framework, Workflow Framework, and Service Management Framework.
