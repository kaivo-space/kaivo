# 1. Purpose

The Operations Principles define the canonical principles governing the Operations domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and long-term stability across all Operations artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operations Principles define:

* Operations principles.
* Operations consistency requirements.
* Operations conformance expectations.
* Operations boundaries.

The Operations Principles do not define:

* Operational procedures.
* Workflow implementations.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Operations Principles

## Principle 1 — Canonical Consistency

Operations artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 2 — Semantic Integrity

Operations shall preserve the approved meaning of operational concepts and relationships.

---

## Principle 3 — Traceability

Operational activities and relationships shall remain traceable across the Operations domain.

---

## Principle 4 — Reusability

Operations structures shall maximize reuse without redefining approved canonical concepts.

---

## Principle 5 — Stability

Approved Operations concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 6 — Scalability

Operations structures shall support sustainable growth while preserving canonical consistency.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Operations shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Operations Requirements

Every Operations artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved canonical artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Operations Principles do not define:

* Business process implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Organizational policy.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Operations shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C2

Operations artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Operations artifacts shall maintain traceability.

---

### C4

Operations artifacts shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C5

Operations artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Operations artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Operations artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Conformance

An Operations artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Operations Vision.
