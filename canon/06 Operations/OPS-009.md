# 1. Purpose

The Operations Catalog defines the canonical catalog of operational assets within the Operations domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes an authoritative structure for organizing, classifying, and referencing operational capabilities while preserving semantic consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operations Catalog defines:

* Operational asset organization.
* Catalog principles.
* Catalog responsibilities.
* Catalog relationships.
* Conformance requirements.

The Operations Catalog does not define:

* Operational implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Catalog Principles

The Operations Catalog shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Structured.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Catalog Organization

The Operations Catalog organizes canonical operational assets into a coherent and authoritative collection.

Every operational asset included in the catalog shall:

* Possess a canonical identity.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain traceable relationships.
* Conform to governing Operations artifacts.

The catalog provides organization and reference only; it does not redefine or duplicate operational assets.

---

# 5. Catalog Relationships

The Operations Catalog shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Operations Vision.
* Conform to the Operations Principles.
* Conform to the Operations Architecture.
* Conform to the Process Framework.
* Conform to the Workflow Framework.
* Conform to the Service Management Framework.
* Conform to the Quality Management Framework.
* Conform to the Operational Governance.

---

# 6. Catalog Responsibilities

The Operations Catalog shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain operational traceability.
* Support discoverability of canonical operational assets.
* Preserve compatibility with governing Operations artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Operations Catalog does not define:

* Workflow implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Operational tooling.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every cataloged operational asset shall possess one canonical identity.

---

### C2

The Operations Catalog shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

The Operations Catalog shall preserve semantic integrity.

---

### C4

The Operations Catalog shall preserve traceability across operational assets.

---

### C5

The Operations Catalog shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Operations Catalog shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Operations Catalog shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Operations Catalog conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves canonical identities.
* Maintains semantic integrity.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains operational traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Operations Vision, Operations Principles, Operations Architecture, Process Framework, Workflow Framework, Service Management Framework, Quality Management Framework, and Operational Governance.
