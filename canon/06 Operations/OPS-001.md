# OPS-001 — Operations Vision

**Document ID:** OPS-001
**Artifact Type:** Core Operations
**Status:** Production Candidate
**Authority:** Kaivo Core
**Version:** 1.0

**Depends On:**

* CORE-000 — Core Ontology
* CORE-001 — Core Asset Model

---

# 1. Purpose

The Operations Vision defines the canonical vision for the Operations domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the long-term conceptual direction for Operations and provides the architectural foundation for all Operations-related artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operations Vision defines:

* The purpose of the Operations domain.
* Operations objectives.
* Operations principles.
* Operations boundaries.
* Conformance expectations.

The Operations Vision does not define:

* Operational procedures.
* Workflow implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Vision

The Operations domain provides the canonical foundation for organizing, coordinating, and governing operational activities within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a consistent conceptual model that enables operations to remain structured, traceable, reusable, and aligned with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 4. Operations Objectives

The Operations domain aims to:

* Preserve operational consistency.
* Support reusable operational structures.
* Maintain traceable operational relationships.
* Enable coherent operational organization.
* Promote long-term maintainability.

---

# 5. Operations Principles

Operations shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Scalable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Operations Relationships

The Operations Vision establishes the conceptual direction for Operations artifacts.

Operations shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Core artifacts.

Operations shall not redefine concepts established by governing Core artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Operations Vision does not define:

* Business processes.
* Organizational governance.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Operations shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C2

Operations shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Operations shall preserve traceability across related artifacts.

---

### C4

Operations shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

Operations shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

Operations shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Operations artifact conforms to this vision if it:

* Preserves semantic consistency.
* Maintains traceable operational relationships.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
