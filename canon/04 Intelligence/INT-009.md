# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Catalog defines the canonical catalog of knowledge assets within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes an authoritative structure for organizing and referencing knowledge assets while preserving semantic consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Knowledge Catalog defines:

* Knowledge asset organization.
* Knowledge catalog principles.
* Catalog responsibilities.
* Catalog relationships.
* Conformance requirements.

The Knowledge Catalog does not define:

* Knowledge content.
* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Repository implementation.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Catalog Principles

The Knowledge Catalog shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Structured.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Catalog Organization

The Knowledge Catalog organizes canonical knowledge assets into a coherent and authoritative collection.

Every knowledge asset included in the catalog shall:

* Possess a canonical identity.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain traceable relationships.
* Conform to governing Intelligence artifacts.

The catalog provides organization and reference only; it does not redefine or duplicate knowledge assets.

---

# 5. Catalog Relationships

The Knowledge Catalog shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Conform to the Knowledge Architecture.
* Conform to the Knowledge Lifecycle.
* Conform to the Decision Framework.
* Conform to the Reasoning Framework.
* Conform to the Investigation Framework.
* Conform to the Knowledge Governance.

---

# 6. Catalog Responsibilities

The Knowledge Catalog shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain knowledge traceability.
* Support discoverability of canonical knowledge assets.
* Preserve compatibility with governing Intelligence artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Knowledge Catalog does not define:

* Knowledge implementation.
* Repository implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Machine learning models.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every cataloged knowledge asset shall possess one canonical identity.

---

### C2

The Knowledge Catalog shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

The Knowledge Catalog shall preserve semantic integrity.

---

### C4

The Knowledge Catalog shall preserve traceability across knowledge assets.

---

### C5

The Knowledge Catalog shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Knowledge Catalog shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Knowledge Catalog shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A Knowledge Catalog conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves canonical identities.
* Maintains semantic integrity.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains knowledge traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision, Intelligence Principles, Knowledge Architecture, Knowledge Lifecycle, Decision Framework, Reasoning Framework, Investigation Framework, and Knowledge Governance.
