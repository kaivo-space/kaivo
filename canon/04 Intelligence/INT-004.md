# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Lifecycle defines the canonical lifecycle of knowledge within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual lifecycle through which knowledge is created, organized, maintained, evolved, and preserved while ensuring semantic integrity, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Knowledge Lifecycle defines:

* Knowledge lifecycle principles.
* Lifecycle stages.
* Lifecycle responsibilities.
* Lifecycle boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Knowledge Lifecycle does not define:

* Runtime behavior.
* Knowledge processing algorithms.
* Artificial intelligence implementation.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Lifecycle Principles

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Governed.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Lifecycle Stages

Every canonical knowledge asset progresses through the following conceptual lifecycle:

## Definition

Knowledge is conceptually defined according to the Core Ontology and governing Intelligence artifacts.

---

## Organization

Knowledge is organized into coherent architectural structures while preserving semantic consistency.

---

## Maintenance

Knowledge is maintained while preserving canonical terminology, approved relationships, and architectural integrity.

---

## Evolution

Knowledge evolves through the approved governance process without redefining approved canonical concepts.

---

## Preservation

Knowledge remains authoritative, traceable, and reusable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 5. Lifecycle Responsibilities

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall:

* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Maintain traceability.
* Preserve compatibility with governing Intelligence artifacts.

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Lifecycle Boundaries

The Knowledge Lifecycle does not define:

* Knowledge inference engines.
* Machine learning models.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every knowledge asset shall follow one canonical lifecycle.

---

### C2

Knowledge shall preserve semantic consistency throughout its lifecycle.

---

### C3

Lifecycle evolution shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Lifecycle governance shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Knowledge Lifecycle shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

A knowledge implementation conforms to this lifecycle if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision, Intelligence Principles, and Knowledge Architecture.
