# 1. Purpose

The Intelligence Principles define the canonical principles governing the Intelligence domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve semantic integrity, consistency, traceability, and long-term stability across all Intelligence artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Principles define:

* Intelligence principles.
* Intelligence consistency requirements.
* Intelligence conformance expectations.
* Intelligence boundaries.

The Intelligence Principles do not define:

* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.
* Operational procedures.

---

# 3. Intelligence Principles

## Principle 1 — Semantic Integrity

Intelligence shall preserve the canonical meaning of every concept and relationship.

---

## Principle 2 — Canonical Consistency

Intelligence artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 3 — Traceability

Knowledge, relationships, and reasoning shall remain traceable across the Intelligence domain.

---

## Principle 4 — Modularity

Intelligence capabilities shall be organized into cohesive and reusable components.

---

## Principle 5 — Reusability

Intelligence structures shall maximize reuse without redefining approved concepts.

---

## Principle 6 — Stability

Approved Intelligence concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Intelligence shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Intelligence Requirements

Every Intelligence artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved canonical artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Intelligence Principles do not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Inference engines.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 7. Conformance

An Intelligence artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision.
