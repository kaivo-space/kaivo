---

# 1. Purpose

The Intelligence Vision defines the canonical vision for Intelligence within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the long-term conceptual direction for the Intelligence domain and provides the architectural foundation for all Intelligence-related artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Vision defines:

* The purpose of Intelligence.
* Intelligence objectives.
* Intelligence principles.
* Intelligence boundaries.
* Conformance expectations.

The Intelligence Vision does not define:

* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Infrastructure.
* User interface behavior.

---

# 3. Vision

Intelligence is the canonical capability responsible for transforming information into structured knowledge that supports understanding, reasoning, and decision support within Kaivo Core.

The Intelligence domain provides a consistent conceptual foundation for organizing, interpreting, and relating knowledge while preserving semantic integrity and architectural consistency.

---

# 4. Intelligence Objectives

The Intelligence domain aims to:

* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Enable reusable knowledge structures.
* Support traceable reasoning.
* Maintain coherent relationships.
* Promote long-term maintainability.

---

# 5. Intelligence Principles

Intelligence shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Modular.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Intelligence Relationships

The Intelligence Vision establishes the conceptual direction for Intelligence artifacts.

Intelligence shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Core artifacts.

Intelligence shall not redefine concepts established by governing Core artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Intelligence Vision does not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Decision algorithms.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Intelligence shall preserve canonical terminology.

---

### C2

Intelligence shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Intelligence shall preserve traceability across related artifacts.

---

### C4

Intelligence shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

Intelligence shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

Intelligence shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Intelligence artifact conforms to this vision if it:

* Preserves semantic consistency.
* Maintains traceable relationships.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
