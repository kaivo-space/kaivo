# 1. Purpose

The Intelligence Index provides the canonical index of Intelligence artifacts within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a single authoritative reference for locating, organizing, and understanding all canonical Intelligence artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, and architectural integrity.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Index defines:

* The canonical Intelligence artifact catalog.
* Intelligence artifact organization.
* Intelligence reference relationships.
* Intelligence index responsibilities.

The Intelligence Index does not define:

* Intelligence semantics.
* Architectural principles.
* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Repository implementation.

# 4. Responsibilities

The Intelligence Index shall:

* Provide an authoritative catalog of Intelligence artifacts.
* Preserve canonical identifiers.
* Preserve canonical artifact names.
* Maintain traceability across the Intelligence domain.
* Support discovery of approved Intelligence artifacts.

The Intelligence Index shall not redefine or replace the artifacts it references.

---

# 5. Organization Principles

The Intelligence Index shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Reusable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Intelligence Index does not:

* Replace normative Intelligence artifacts.
* Introduce new Intelligence concepts.
* Redefine canonical terminology.
* Modify approved Intelligence artifacts.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Intelligence artifact shall appear at most once in the Intelligence Index.

---

### C2

The Intelligence Index shall preserve canonical identifiers.

---

### C3

The Intelligence Index shall preserve canonical artifact names.

---

### C4

The Intelligence Index shall remain consistent with approved Intelligence artifacts.

---

### C5

The Intelligence Index shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Intelligence Index shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

An implementation conforms to the Intelligence Index if it:

* References approved Intelligence artifacts accurately.
* Preserves canonical identifiers.
* Maintains consistency with approved Intelligence artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
