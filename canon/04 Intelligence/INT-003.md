---

# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Architecture defines the canonical architectural organization of knowledge within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual structure, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize knowledge while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Knowledge Architecture defines:

* Knowledge architectural organization.
* Knowledge responsibilities.
* Knowledge relationships.
* Knowledge boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Knowledge Architecture does not define:

* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Knowledge inference mechanisms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 3. Architectural Principles

The Knowledge Architecture shall be:

* Canonical.
* Modular.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Architectural Organization

Knowledge is organized as a coherent collection of canonical concepts, relationships, and supporting artifacts.

Each knowledge artifact fulfills one distinct architectural responsibility while contributing to a unified knowledge architecture.

The Knowledge Architecture preserves these responsibilities without overlap or duplication.

---

# 5. Architectural Relationships

Knowledge artifacts shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

Knowledge artifacts shall not redefine responsibilities established by other canonical artifacts.

---

# 6. Architectural Responsibilities

The Knowledge Architecture shall:

* Organize knowledge into coherent architectural structures.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Preserve traceability across the Intelligence domain.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Knowledge Architecture does not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Knowledge processing algorithms.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Repository implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every knowledge artifact shall have exactly one architectural responsibility.

---

### C2

Knowledge responsibilities shall not overlap.

---

### C3

Knowledge artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Knowledge artifacts shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C5

The Knowledge Architecture shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Knowledge Architecture shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Knowledge Architecture shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A knowledge artifact conforms to the Knowledge Architecture if it:

* Preserves its defined architectural responsibility.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Preserves approved dependency relationships.
* Preserves canonical terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision and Intelligence Principles.
