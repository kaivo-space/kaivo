# 1. Purpose

The Investigation Framework defines the canonical framework governing investigation within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to support structured investigation while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, consistency, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Investigation Framework defines:

* Investigation principles.
* Investigation responsibilities.
* Investigation relationships.
* Investigation boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Investigation Framework does not define:

* Investigation methodologies.
* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 3. Investigation Principles

The Investigation Framework shall be:

* Canonical.
* Structured.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Explainable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Investigation Organization

Investigation shall operate on approved canonical knowledge and preserve semantic consistency throughout the investigation process.

Every investigation shall maintain compatibility with governing Intelligence artifacts and preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

# 5. Investigation Relationships

The Investigation Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Conform to the Knowledge Architecture.
* Conform to the Knowledge Lifecycle.
* Conform to the Decision Framework.
* Conform to the Reasoning Framework.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Investigation Framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Investigation Responsibilities

The Investigation Framework shall:

* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain investigation traceability.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Support reusable investigation structures.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Investigation Framework does not define:

* Investigation software.
* Machine learning models.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every investigation shall be based on approved canonical knowledge.

---

### C2

Investigations shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Investigations shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Investigation relationships shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Investigation Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Investigation Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Investigation Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An investigation implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains investigation traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision, Intelligence Principles, Knowledge Architecture, Knowledge Lifecycle, Decision Framework, and Reasoning Framework.
