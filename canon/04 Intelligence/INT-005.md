# 1. Purpose

The Decision Framework defines the canonical framework governing decision formation within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to support consistent, traceable, and implementation-neutral decision making.

---

# 2. Scope

The Decision Framework defines:

* Decision principles.
* Decision responsibilities.
* Decision relationships.
* Decision boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Decision Framework does not define:

* Decision algorithms.
* Artificial intelligence implementations.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Decision Principles

The Decision Framework shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Explainable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Decision Organization

Decisions shall be derived from approved knowledge while preserving semantic consistency and canonical terminology.

Every decision shall maintain compatibility with the governing Intelligence artifacts.

---

# 5. Decision Relationships

The Decision Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Conform to the Knowledge Architecture.
* Conform to the Knowledge Lifecycle.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Decision Framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Decision Responsibilities

The Decision Framework shall:

* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain decision traceability.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Support reusable decision structures.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Decision Framework does not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Optimization algorithms.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every decision shall be based on approved canonical knowledge.

---

### C2

Decisions shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Decisions shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Decision relationships shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Decision Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Decision Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Decision Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A decision implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains decision traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision, Intelligence Principles, Knowledge Architecture, and Knowledge Lifecycle.
