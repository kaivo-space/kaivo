# 1. Purpose

The Reasoning Framework defines the canonical framework governing reasoning within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to support consistent, traceable, explainable, and implementation-neutral reasoning.

---

# 2. Scope

The Reasoning Framework defines:

* Reasoning principles.
* Reasoning responsibilities.
* Reasoning relationships.
* Reasoning boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Reasoning Framework does not define:

* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Inference engines.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Reasoning Principles

The Reasoning Framework shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Explainable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Reasoning Organization

Reasoning shall operate upon approved canonical knowledge while preserving semantic consistency and approved terminology.

Every reasoning activity shall remain compatible with governing Intelligence artifacts and maintain architectural integrity.

---

# 5. Reasoning Relationships

The Reasoning Framework shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Conform to the Knowledge Architecture.
* Conform to the Knowledge Lifecycle.
* Conform to the Decision Framework.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

The Reasoning Framework shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Reasoning Responsibilities

The Reasoning Framework shall:

* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain reasoning traceability.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Support reusable reasoning structures.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Reasoning Framework does not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Optimization algorithms.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every reasoning process shall be based on approved canonical knowledge.

---

### C2

Reasoning shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Reasoning shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Reasoning relationships shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

The Reasoning Framework shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Reasoning Framework shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Reasoning Framework shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A reasoning implementation conforms to this framework if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains reasoning traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Intelligence Vision, Intelligence Principles, Knowledge Architecture, Knowledge Lifecycle, and Decision Framework.
