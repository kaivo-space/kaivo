# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Governance defines the canonical governance framework for knowledge within the Intelligence domain of Kaivo Core.

It establishes the governance principles, responsibilities, and constraints required to preserve semantic integrity, consistency, traceability, and long-term stability while remaining implementation-neutral.

---

# 2. Scope

The Knowledge Governance defines:

* Knowledge governance principles.
* Governance responsibilities.
* Governance relationships.
* Governance boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Knowledge Governance does not define:

* Knowledge implementation.
* Artificial intelligence algorithms.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Operational procedures.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Knowledge Governance shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Verifiable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Governance Responsibilities

Knowledge Governance shall:

* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Preserve approved architectural relationships.
* Maintain knowledge traceability.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Intelligence artifacts.

Knowledge Governance shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 5. Governance Relationships

Knowledge Governance shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Intelligence Vision.
* Conform to the Intelligence Principles.
* Conform to the Knowledge Architecture.
* Conform to the Knowledge Lifecycle.
* Conform to the Decision Framework.
* Conform to the Reasoning Framework.
* Conform to the Investigation Framework.

Governance relationships shall preserve the integrity of participating artifacts.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The Knowledge Governance does not define:

* Machine learning models.
* Knowledge processing engines.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.
