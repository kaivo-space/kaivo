# 1. Purpose

The Infrastructure Principles define the canonical principles governing the Infrastructure domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and long-term stability across all Infrastructure artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Infrastructure Principles define:

* Infrastructure principles.
* Infrastructure consistency requirements.
* Infrastructure conformance expectations.
* Infrastructure boundaries.

The Infrastructure Principles do not define:

* Infrastructure implementation.
* Deployment procedures.
* Runtime behavior.
* Vendor technologies.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Infrastructure Principles

## Principle 1 — Canonical Consistency

Infrastructure artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 2 — Semantic Integrity

Infrastructure shall preserve the approved meaning of infrastructure concepts and relationships.

---

## Principle 3 — Traceability

Infrastructure resources and relationships shall remain traceable across the Infrastructure domain.

---

## Principle 4 — Reusability

Infrastructure structures shall maximize reuse without redefining approved canonical concepts.

---

## Principle 5 — Stability

Approved Infrastructure concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 6 — Scalability

Infrastructure structures shall support sustainable growth while preserving canonical consistency.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Infrastructure shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Infrastructure Requirements

Every Infrastructure artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved canonical artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Infrastructure Principles do not define:

* Runtime operations.
* Platform selection.
* Infrastructure automation.
* Vendor-specific technologies.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Infrastructure shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C2

Infrastructure artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Infrastructure artifacts shall maintain traceability.

---

### C4

---

### C5

Infrastructure artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Infrastructure artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

---

# 7. Conformance

An Infrastructure artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Infrastructure Vision.
