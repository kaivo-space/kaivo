# 1. Purpose

The Infrastructure Standards define the canonical standards governing the Infrastructure domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to preserve consistency, interoperability, traceability, and implementation neutrality across all Infrastructure artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Infrastructure Standards define:

* Infrastructure standardization principles.
* Infrastructure standard responsibilities.
* Infrastructure standard relationships.
* Infrastructure standard boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Infrastructure Standards do not define:

* Technical specifications.
* Vendor technologies.
* Runtime execution.
* Deployment procedures.
* Infrastructure implementation.

---

# 3. Standardization Principles

Infrastructure Standards shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Interoperable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Standard Organization

Infrastructure Standards establish the canonical rules that govern Infrastructure artifacts.

Every Infrastructure Standard shall:

* Possess one defined canonical purpose.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain traceable relationships.
* Support interoperability across Infrastructure artifacts.
* Maintain architectural consistency.

---

# 5. Standard Relationships

Infrastructure Standards shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Infrastructure Vision.
* Conform to the Infrastructure Principles.
* Conform to the Infrastructure Architecture.
* Conform to the Infrastructure Components.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

Infrastructure Standards shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Standard Responsibilities

Infrastructure Standards shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain standards traceability.
* Support reusable infrastructure structures.
* Maintain architectural consistency across Infrastructure artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

Infrastructure Standards do not define:

* Infrastructure implementations.
* Vendor-specific technologies.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Deployment automation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Infrastructure Standard shall possess one canonical purpose.

---

### C2

Infrastructure Standards shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Infrastructure Standards shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Infrastructure Standards shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

Infrastructure Standards shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Infrastructure Standards shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Infrastructure Standards shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Infrastructure Standard conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves its canonical purpose.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains standards traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Infrastructure Vision, Infrastructure Principles, Infrastructure Architecture, and Infrastructure Components.
