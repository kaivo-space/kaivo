# 1. Purpose

The Roles & Responsibilities document defines the canonical governance responsibilities required for the creation, review, approval, publication, and maintenance of Kaivo Core artifacts.

It establishes responsibility boundaries while preserving the integrity of the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

This document defines:

* Governance roles.
* Governance responsibilities.
* Responsibility boundaries.
* Accountability principles.

This document does not define:

* Organizational structure.
* Project management.
* Architecture decisions.
* Implementation procedures.
* Runtime behavior.

---

# 3. Governance Roles

The governance lifecycle recognizes the following canonical roles:

* Executive
* Architect
* Reviewer
* Producer
* Publisher

Each role has a distinct responsibility within the artifact lifecycle.

---

# 4. Executive Responsibilities

The Executive shall:

* Determine what artifacts are required.
* Define production priorities.
* Resolve canonical conflicts.
* Approve architectural direction.

The Executive shall not perform publication activities as part of this governance model.

---

# 5. Architect Responsibilities

The Architect shall:

* Define and maintain the Core Architecture.
* Preserve architectural consistency.
* Define canonical relationships.
* Prevent architectural conflicts.

The Architect shall not modify approved artifacts outside the approved governance process.

---

# 6. Reviewer Responsibilities

The Reviewer shall:

* Review artifacts for correctness.
* Verify consistency with approved artifacts.
* Verify canonical terminology.
* Recommend approval or rejection.

The Reviewer shall not redesign approved architecture.

---

# 7. Producer Responsibilities

The Producer shall:

* Produce publication-ready artifacts.
* Preserve approved architecture.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Follow approved dependencies.
* Prepare artifacts for publication.

The Producer shall not redesign architecture or resolve canonical conflicts independently.

---

# 8. Publisher Responsibilities

The Publisher shall:

* Publish approved artifacts.
* Preserve approved content.
* Maintain repository integrity.
* Preserve artifact identity during publication.

The Publisher shall not modify approved artifact content.

---

# 9. Responsibility Boundaries

Governance responsibilities shall remain clearly separated.

No governance role shall:

* Redesign approved architecture.
* Redefine canonical terminology.
* Modify approved artifacts outside the approved governance process.
* Override Executive decisions.

---

# 10. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Governance responsibilities shall remain explicitly defined.

---

### C2

Every governance role shall preserve approved canonical artifacts.

---

### C3

Canonical terminology shall not be redefined.

---

### C4

Approved architecture shall not be redesigned through governance activities.

---

### C5

All governance roles shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

All governance roles shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 11. Conformance

A governance implementation conforms to this document if it:

* Maintains the defined governance responsibilities.
* Preserves approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintains canonical terminology.
* Conforms to the Governance Charter.
* Conforms to the Canon Principles.
