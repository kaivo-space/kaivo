# 1. Purpose

The Architectural Decision Record (ADR) Standard defines the canonical requirements for documenting architectural decisions within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a consistent approach for recording architectural decisions while preserving traceability, governance, and compatibility with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

The ADR Standard defines:

* Architectural decision documentation principles.
* ADR structure requirements.
* ADR governance requirements.
* ADR boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The ADR Standard does not define:

* Architectural decisions themselves.
* Architecture redesign.
* Technical implementation.
* Repository workflows.
* Runtime behavior.

---

# 3. ADR Principles

Architectural Decision Records shall be:

* Canonical.
* Traceable.
* Consistent.
* Verifiable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. ADR Requirements

Every Architectural Decision Record shall:

* Possess a unique identifier.
* Clearly identify the architectural decision being recorded.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved artifacts.
* Preserve decision traceability.

---

# 5. Documentation Requirements

Every ADR shall document:

* The decision being recorded.
* The governing authority for the decision.
* Applicable canonical references.
* The lifecycle status of the decision record.

The documentation structure shall conform to the Documentation Standard.

---

# 6. Governance Requirements

Architectural Decision Records shall:

* Follow the Governance Charter.
* Respect the Decision Authority Model.
* Preserve approved canonical artifacts.
* Remain compatible with the Document Lifecycle.
* Preserve architectural traceability.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The ADR Standard does not define:

* Architecture planning.
* Executive prioritization.
* Implementation details.
* Repository implementation.
* Runtime execution.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every ADR shall possess exactly one canonical identifier.

---

### C2

Every ADR shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Every ADR shall preserve architectural traceability.

---

### C4

ADR documentation shall conform to the Documentation Standard.

---

### C5

ADR governance shall conform to the Decision Authority Model.

---

### C6

The ADR Standard shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C7

The ADR Standard shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 9. Conformance

An Architectural Decision Record conforms to this standard if it:

* Applies the required ADR structure.
* Preserves architectural traceability.
* Uses approved canonical terminology.
* Maintains compatibility with approved artifacts.
* Conforms to the Governance Charter.
* Conforms to the Documentation Standard.
