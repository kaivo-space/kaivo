# 1. Purpose

The Canon Principles define the fundamental principles that govern the creation, maintenance, publication, and evolution of the Kaivo Canon.

They establish the normative rules required to preserve the integrity and consistency of all canonical artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Canon Principles define:

* Canonical principles.
* Canon preservation rules.
* Consistency requirements.
* Conformance expectations.

The Canon Principles do not define:

* Architecture redesign.
* Project planning.
* Implementation strategies.
* Runtime behavior.
* Organizational procedures.

---

# 3. Canon Principles

## Principle 1 — Canonical Authority

Approved canonical artifacts are the authoritative source of truth.

---

## Principle 2 — Consistency

Canonical terminology shall remain consistent across all artifacts.

---

## Principle 3 — Integrity

Artifacts shall preserve the meaning and relationships established by approved canonical artifacts.

---

## Principle 4 — Stability

Approved canonical artifacts shall remain stable unless superseded through an authorized governance process.

---

## Principle 5 — Reusability

Canonical artifacts shall be designed for reuse across Kaivo without redefining established concepts.

---

## Principle 6 — Traceability

Canonical artifacts shall remain uniquely identifiable and traceable throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Canonical artifacts shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Canon Responsibilities

Every canonical artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain consistency with approved artifacts.
* Respect established dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Canon Principles do not authorize:

* Redefinition of approved concepts.
* Introduction of conflicting terminology.
* Removal of approved canonical concepts.
* Architectural redesign.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Approved canonical artifacts shall be treated as authoritative.

---

### C2

Canonical terminology shall not be redefined.

---

### C3

New artifacts shall remain consistent with approved artifacts.

---

### C4

Canonical relationships shall be preserved.

---

### C5

All canonical artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

All canonical artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Canonical artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Conformance

An artifact conforms to the Canon Principles if it:

* Preserves approved canonical terminology.
* Maintains consistency with approved artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserves established canonical relationships.
