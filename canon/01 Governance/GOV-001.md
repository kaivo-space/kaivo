# 1. Purpose

The Governance Charter defines the canonical governance principles for Kaivo Core.

It establishes the responsibilities, authority, and governance boundaries required to preserve the integrity, consistency, and continuity of the Kaivo Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

The Governance Charter defines:

* Governance objectives.
* Governance principles.
* Governance responsibilities.
* Governance boundaries.
* Canonical governance constraints.

The Governance Charter does not define:

* Project planning.
* Product roadmaps.
* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Organizational management.

---

# 3. Governance Objectives

Governance exists to:

* Preserve architectural integrity.
* Maintain canonical consistency.
* Ensure artifact quality.
* Protect approved terminology.
* Support long-term maintainability.

---

# 4. Governance Principles

Governance shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Transparent.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 5. Governance Responsibilities

Governance shall:

* Preserve approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintain consistency across Core artifacts.
* Protect canonical terminology.
* Ensure conformance with the Core Ontology and Core Asset Model.

Governance shall not redesign or redefine approved artifacts.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The Governance Charter does not define:

* Executive prioritization.
* Architecture redesign.
* Implementation decisions.
* Operational procedures.
* Deployment processes.

These concerns belong to their respective authoritative artifacts or decision makers.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Governance shall preserve approved canonical artifacts.

---

### C2

Governance shall not redefine canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Governance shall maintain consistency across Core artifacts.

---

### C4

Governance shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

Governance shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

Governance shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

An implementation conforms to this charter if it:

* Preserves approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintains canonical terminology.
* Maintains consistency across Core artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
