# 1. Purpose

The Change Management Policy defines the canonical principles governing changes to Kaivo Core artifacts.

It establishes the governance requirements for introducing, reviewing, approving, and applying changes while preserving the integrity of the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

The Change Management Policy defines:

* Change governance principles.
* Change lifecycle requirements.
* Change conformance requirements.
* Change boundaries.

The Change Management Policy does not define:

* Architecture redesign.
* Project planning.
* Repository workflows.
* Deployment procedures.
* Runtime behavior.

---

# 3. Change Management Principles

Change management shall be:

* Canonical.
* Controlled.
* Traceable.
* Consistent.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Change Requirements

Every proposed change shall:

* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain consistency with approved artifacts.
* Respect established dependencies.
* Follow the approved governance process.
* Preserve artifact traceability.

---

# 5. Governance Requirements

Changes shall:

* Be evaluated before approval.
* Respect defined governance responsibilities.
* Preserve the integrity of approved artifacts.
* Conform to the Governance Charter.
* Conform to the Document Lifecycle.
* Conform to the Versioning Policy.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Change Management Policy does not define:

* Organizational procedures.
* Release schedules.
* Technical implementation.
* Repository operations.
* Runtime execution.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Changes shall preserve approved canonical artifacts.

---

### C2

Changes shall not redefine canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Changes shall maintain compatibility with approved artifacts.

---

### C4

Changes shall follow the approved governance process.

---

### C5

Changes shall preserve artifact traceability.

---

### C6

The Change Management Policy shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C7

The Change Management Policy shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

A governance implementation conforms to this policy if it:

* Applies controlled change management.
* Preserves approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintains traceability.
* Conforms to the Governance Charter.
* Conforms to the Document Lifecycle.
* Conforms to the Versioning Policy.
