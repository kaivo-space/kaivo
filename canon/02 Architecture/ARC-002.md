# 1. Purpose

The Architecture Principles define the canonical principles that govern the design, organization, maintenance, and evolution of the Kaivo Core Architecture.

They establish the normative architectural rules that preserve consistency, integrity, and long-term sustainability across all Core artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Architecture Principles define:

* Architectural principles.
* Architectural consistency requirements.
* Architectural conformance expectations.
* Architectural boundaries.

The Architecture Principles do not define:

* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure.
* Deployment strategies.
* Project planning.

---

# 3. Architecture Principles

## Principle 1 — Canonical Consistency

All architectural artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 2 — Clear Responsibility

Every architectural artifact shall have a single, well-defined responsibility.

---

## Principle 3 — Modularity

Architecture shall be organized into cohesive and reusable components.

---

## Principle 4 — Traceability

Architectural decisions and relationships shall remain traceable throughout the artifact lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 — Stability

Approved architectural concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 6 — Reusability

Architectural components shall maximize reuse without redefining established concepts.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Architecture shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Architectural Requirements

Every architectural artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Respect approved dependencies.
* Maintain consistency with related architectural artifacts.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Architecture Principles do not define:

* Programming languages.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Repository implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Operational procedures.

---

---

---

Architectural artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 7. Conformance

An architectural artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves approved canonical terminology.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Respects approved dependencies.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Architecture Vision.
