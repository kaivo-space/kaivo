# 1. Purpose

The Architecture Layers define the canonical layered organization of the Kaivo Core Architecture.

They establish a consistent architectural structure that organizes Core artifacts into logical layers while preserving responsibility boundaries, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Architecture Layers define:

* Architectural layer organization.
* Layer responsibilities.
* Relationships between layers.
* Layer conformance requirements.
* Architectural boundaries.

The Architecture Layers do not define:

* Technical implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Deployment strategies.
* Programming models.

---

# 3. Layering Principles

The architectural layering shall be:

* Canonical.
* Hierarchical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Modular.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Layer Structure

The Kaivo Core Architecture is organized into the following conceptual layers:

## Foundation Layer

Provides the canonical concepts and structural foundations upon which all higher layers depend.

---

## Governance Layer

Defines the governance principles, policies, and standards that preserve the integrity of the Kaivo Canon.

---

## Capability Layer

Defines reusable Core capabilities through models, specifications, templates, registries, maps, and related artifacts.

---

## System Layer

Organizes related Core capabilities into coherent Systems while preserving the integrity of participating Core Assets.

---

# 5. Layer Relationships

Each architectural layer:

* Depends only on lower foundational layers.
* Preserves the responsibilities of lower layers.
* Shall not redefine concepts established by lower layers.

Layer relationships shall maintain architectural consistency throughout the Kaivo Canon.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Architecture Layers do not define:

* Software architecture.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Repository organization.
* Runtime execution.
* Operational procedures.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every architectural artifact shall belong to one architectural layer.

---

### C2

Higher layers shall preserve concepts established by lower layers.

---

### C3

Architectural layers shall maintain clear responsibility boundaries.

---

### C4

Architectural layers shall preserve canonical consistency.

---

### C5

The Architecture Layers shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Architecture Layers shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Architecture Layers shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

An architectural artifact conforms to the Architecture Layers if it:

* Belongs to an appropriate architectural layer.
* Preserves defined responsibility boundaries.
* Maintains consistency with lower architectural layers.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Architecture Vision and Architecture Principles.
