# 1. Purpose

The Canon Structure defines the canonical organizational structure of the Kaivo Canon.

It establishes the architectural organization of canonical artifacts and their structural relationships while preserving consistency, traceability, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Canon Structure defines:

* Canonical structural organization.
* Structural relationships.
* Structural boundaries.
* Structural conformance requirements.

The Canon Structure does not define:

* Technical implementation.
* Repository implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Deployment strategies.
* Organizational procedures.

---

# 3. Structure Principles

The Canon Structure shall be:

* Canonical.
* Hierarchical.
* Modular.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Canon Organization

The Kaivo Canon is organized into coherent architectural collections.

Each collection groups related canonical artifacts according to their defined responsibilities while preserving clear architectural boundaries.

Each canonical artifact belongs to exactly one collection.

---

# 5. Structural Relationships

The Canon Structure shall:

* Preserve approved architectural layers.
* Preserve approved domains.
* Preserve approved component responsibilities.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.
* Preserve canonical terminology.

Structural relationships shall not redefine responsibilities established by other architectural artifacts.

---

# 6. Structural Integrity

The Canon Structure shall ensure that:

* Every artifact has one canonical location within the architecture.
* Every artifact maintains explicit relationships with its governing artifacts.
* Every structural relationship remains traceable throughout the document lifecycle.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Canon Structure does not define:

* Repository layout.
* Version control workflows.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every canonical artifact shall belong to exactly one structural collection.

---

### C2

Structural organization shall preserve approved responsibility boundaries.

---

### C3

Structural relationships shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C4

The Canon Structure shall preserve canonical terminology.

---

### C5

The Canon Structure shall conform to the Architecture Layers.

---

### C6

The Canon Structure shall conform to the Domain Model.

---

### C7

The Canon Structure shall conform to the Component Model.

---

### C8

The Canon Structure shall conform to the Dependency Rules.

---

### C9

The Canon Structure shall conform to the Boundary Rules.

---

### C10

The Canon Structure shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An architectural artifact conforms to the Canon Structure if it:

* Belongs to one canonical structural collection.
* Preserves approved architectural relationships.
* Maintains structural consistency.
* Preserves canonical terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Architecture Vision, Architecture Principles, Architecture Layers, Domain Model, Component Model, Dependency Rules, and Boundary Rules.
