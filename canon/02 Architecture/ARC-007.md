# 1. Purpose

The Boundary Rules define the canonical principles governing architectural boundaries within Kaivo Core.

They establish the rules that preserve clear responsibility separation, prevent architectural overlap, and maintain the integrity of the approved Kaivo Canon while remaining implementation-neutral.

---

# 2. Scope

The Boundary Rules define:

* Architectural boundary principles.
* Boundary responsibilities.
* Boundary relationships.
* Boundary conformance requirements.

The Boundary Rules do not define:

* Software implementation.
* Runtime behavior.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Deployment strategies.
* Programming models.

---

# 3. Boundary Principles

Architectural boundaries shall be:

* Canonical.
* Explicit.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Boundary Requirements

Every architectural boundary shall:

* Define a clear responsibility.
* Preserve domain ownership.
* Preserve component ownership.
* Prevent responsibility overlap.
* Maintain compatibility with approved architectural artifacts.

---

# 5. Boundary Relationships

Architectural boundaries shall:

* Separate responsibilities between domains.
* Separate responsibilities between components.
* Respect approved dependency relationships.
* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Maintain architectural consistency.

Boundaries shall not redefine, duplicate, or replace approved responsibilities.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Boundary Rules do not define:

* Repository organization.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure boundaries.
* Technical implementation.
* Organizational procedures.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every architectural boundary shall define exactly one responsibility boundary.

---

### C2

Responsibilities shall not overlap across architectural boundaries.

---

### C3

Architectural boundaries shall preserve approved domain ownership.

---

### C4

Architectural boundaries shall preserve approved component ownership.

---

### C5

Architectural boundaries shall conform to the Dependency Rules.

---

### C6

Architectural boundaries shall conform to the Domain Model.

---

### C7

Architectural boundaries shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C8

Architectural boundaries shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C9

Architectural boundaries shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

An architectural artifact conforms to these Boundary Rules if it:

* Preserves defined responsibility boundaries.
* Avoids responsibility overlap.
* Maintains approved dependency relationships.
* Preserves domain and component ownership.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Architecture Vision, Architecture Principles, Architecture Layers, Domain Model, Component Model, and Dependency Rules.
