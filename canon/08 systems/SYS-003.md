# 1. Purpose

The Systems Architecture defines the canonical architectural organization of the Systems domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the conceptual structure, responsibilities, and relationships required to organize systems while preserving semantic integrity, traceability, consistency, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Systems Architecture defines:

* Systems architectural organization.
* Systems responsibilities.
* Systems relationships.
* Systems boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Systems Architecture does not define:

* System implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Vendor technologies.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Architectural Principles

The Systems Architecture shall be:

* Canonical.
* Modular.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Architectural Organization

The Systems domain is organized as a coherent collection of canonical systems and supporting artifacts.

Each Systems artifact fulfills one distinct architectural responsibility while contributing to a unified Systems architecture.

The Systems Architecture preserves these responsibilities without overlap or duplication.

---

# 5. Architectural Relationships

Systems artifacts shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Systems Vision.
* Conform to the Systems Principles.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

Systems artifacts shall not redefine responsibilities established by other canonical artifacts.

---

# 6. Architectural Responsibilities

The Systems Architecture shall:

* Organize systems into coherent architectural structures.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain architectural consistency.
* Preserve traceability across the Systems domain.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Systems Architecture does not define:

* Runtime operations.
* Infrastructure implementation.
* Vendor technologies.
* Platform implementation.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Systems artifact shall have exactly one architectural responsibility.

---

### C2

Systems responsibilities shall not overlap.

---

### C3

Systems artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Systems artifacts shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C5

The Systems Architecture shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

The Systems Architecture shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

The Systems Architecture shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A Systems artifact conforms to the Systems Architecture if it:

* Preserves its defined architectural responsibility.
* Maintains architectural consistency.
* Preserves approved dependency relationships.
* Preserves canonical terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Systems Vision and Systems Principles.
