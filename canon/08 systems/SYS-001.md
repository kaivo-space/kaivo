---

# 1. Purpose

The Systems Vision defines the canonical vision for the Systems domain within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the long-term conceptual direction for Systems and provides the architectural foundation for all Systems-related artifacts while preserving consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and implementation neutrality.

---

# 2. Scope

The Systems Vision defines:

* The purpose of the Systems domain.
* Systems objectives.
* Systems principles.
* Systems boundaries.
* Conformance expectations.

The Systems Vision does not define:

* System implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Technical implementation.
* Platform-specific behavior.

---

# 3. Vision

The Systems domain provides the canonical foundation for representing and organizing systems within Kaivo Core.

It establishes a consistent conceptual model that enables systems to remain structured, traceable, reusable, and aligned with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 4. Systems Objectives

The Systems domain aims to:

* Preserve system consistency.
* Support reusable system structures.
* Maintain traceable system relationships.
* Enable coherent system organization.
* Promote long-term maintainability.

---

# 5. Systems Principles

Systems shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Scalable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 6. Systems Relationships

The Systems Vision establishes the conceptual direction for Systems artifacts.

Systems shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Core artifacts.

Systems shall not redefine concepts established by governing Core artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Systems Vision does not define:

* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Platform implementation.
* Vendor technologies.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Systems shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C2

Systems shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C3

Systems shall preserve traceability across related artifacts.

---

### C4

Systems shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

Systems shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C6

Systems shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A Systems artifact conforms to this vision if it:

* Preserves semantic consistency.
* Maintains traceable system relationships.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
