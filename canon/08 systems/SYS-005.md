# 1. Purpose

The Systems Standards define the canonical standards governing the Systems domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the conceptual principles, responsibilities, and relationships required to preserve consistency, interoperability, traceability, and implementation neutrality across all Systems artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Systems Standards define:

* Systems standardization principles.
* Systems standard responsibilities.
* Systems standard relationships.
* Systems standard boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Systems Standards do not define:

* Technical specifications.
* Platform-specific implementations.
* Runtime execution.
* Vendor technologies.
* System implementation.

---

# 3. Standardization Principles

Systems Standards shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Interoperable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Standard Organization

Systems Standards establish the canonical rules that govern Systems artifacts.

Every Systems Standard shall:

* Possess one defined canonical purpose.
* Preserve semantic consistency.
* Maintain traceable relationships.
* Support interoperability across Systems artifacts.
* Maintain architectural consistency.

---

# 5. Standard Relationships

Systems Standards shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Systems Vision.
* Conform to the Systems Principles.
* Conform to the Systems Architecture.
* Conform to the System Components.
* Preserve approved dependency relationships.

Systems Standards shall not redefine approved canonical concepts.

---

# 6. Standard Responsibilities

Systems Standards shall:

* Preserve canonical terminology.
* Preserve semantic integrity.
* Maintain standards traceability.
* Support reusable system structures.
* Maintain architectural consistency across Systems artifacts.

---

# 7. Boundaries

Systems Standards do not define:

* System implementation.
* Vendor-specific technologies.
* Runtime execution.
* Repository implementation.
* Infrastructure architecture.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every Systems Standard shall possess one canonical purpose.

---

### C2

Systems Standards shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Systems Standards shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

Systems Standards shall preserve approved architectural relationships.

---

### C5

Systems Standards shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Systems Standards shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Systems Standards shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

A Systems Standard conforms to this document if it:

* Preserves its canonical purpose.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Maintains standards traceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Systems Vision, Systems Principles, Systems Architecture, and System Components.
