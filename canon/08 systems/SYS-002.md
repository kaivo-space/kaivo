# 1. Purpose

The Systems Principles define the canonical principles governing the Systems domain within Kaivo Core.

They establish the normative rules that preserve consistency, traceability, semantic integrity, and long-term stability across all Systems artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Systems Principles define:

* Systems principles.
* Systems consistency requirements.
* Systems conformance expectations.
* Systems boundaries.

The Systems Principles do not define:

* System implementation.
* Runtime execution.
* Infrastructure.
* Vendor technologies.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 3. Systems Principles

## Principle 1 — Canonical Consistency

Systems artifacts shall remain consistent with the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

## Principle 2 — Semantic Integrity

Systems shall preserve the approved meaning of system concepts and relationships.

---

## Principle 3 — Traceability

System relationships and dependencies shall remain traceable across the Systems domain.

---

## Principle 4 — Reusability

Systems structures shall maximize reuse without redefining approved canonical concepts.

---

## Principle 5 — Stability

Approved Systems concepts shall remain stable unless changed through the approved governance process.

---

## Principle 6 — Scalability

Systems structures shall support sustainable growth while preserving canonical consistency.

---

## Principle 7 — Implementation Neutrality

Systems shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 4. Systems Requirements

Every Systems artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain compatibility with approved canonical artifacts.
* Respect approved dependencies.

---

# 5. Boundaries

The Systems Principles do not define:

* Runtime operations.
* Infrastructure architecture.
* Vendor technologies.
* System implementation.
* Technical implementation.

---

# 6. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Systems shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C2

Systems artifacts shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Systems artifacts shall maintain traceability.

---

### C4

Systems artifacts shall preserve approved dependency relationships.

---

### C5

Systems artifacts shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C6

Systems artifacts shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

### C7

Systems artifacts shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Conformance

A Systems artifact conforms to these principles if it:

* Preserves semantic integrity.
* Maintains canonical consistency.
* Preserves traceability.
* Preserves approved terminology.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Systems Vision.
