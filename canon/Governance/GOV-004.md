# 1. Purpose

The Decision Authority Model defines the canonical authority boundaries for decisions within Kaivo Core.

It establishes which governance roles are responsible for making, validating, approving, and executing decisions while preserving the integrity of the approved Kaivo Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

The Decision Authority Model defines:

* Decision authority.
* Decision ownership.
* Decision responsibilities.
* Authority boundaries.
* Decision conformance principles.

The Decision Authority Model does not define:

* Project planning.
* Organizational structure.
* Runtime behavior.
* Technical implementation.
* Architecture redesign.

---

# 3. Decision Authority Principles

Decision authority shall be:

* Canonical.
* Explicit.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

Authority shall be exercised only within the responsibilities defined by the Governance Charter and the Roles & Responsibilities document.

---

# 4. Decision Authority

## Executive

The Executive has authority to:

* Determine which artifacts are produced.
* Define production priorities.
* Resolve canonical conflicts.
* Approve architectural direction.

---

## Architect

The Architect has authority to:

* Define and maintain the Core Architecture.
* Define canonical relationships.
* Maintain architectural consistency.

---

## Reviewer

The Reviewer has authority to:

* Validate artifact correctness.
* Verify consistency with approved artifacts.
* Recommend approval or rejection.

---

## Producer

The Producer has authority to:

* Produce publication-ready artifacts.
* Execute approved production work.
* Preserve canonical terminology and dependencies.

---

## Publisher

The Publisher has authority to:

* Publish approved artifacts.
* Preserve approved content.
* Maintain repository integrity.

---

# 5. Authority Boundaries

No governance role shall:

* Redesign approved architecture.
* Redefine canonical terminology.
* Modify approved artifacts outside the approved governance process.
* Exercise authority assigned to another governance role.

---

# 6. Decision Conformance

Every governance decision shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical artifacts.
* Respect established authority boundaries.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Decision authority shall be explicitly defined.

---

### C2

Authority shall remain consistent with approved governance artifacts.

---

### C3

Authority shall not redefine canonical terminology.

---

### C4

Authority shall not redesign approved architecture.

---

### C5

Governance decisions shall preserve approved canonical artifacts.

---

### C6

Decision authority shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 8. Conformance

A governance implementation conforms to this model if it:

* Applies authority according to this model.
* Preserves approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintains authority boundaries.
* Conforms to the Governance Charter.
* Conforms to the Canon Principles.
* Conforms to the Roles & Responsibilities document.
