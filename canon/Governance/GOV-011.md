# 1. Purpose

The Glossary defines the canonical vocabulary used throughout Kaivo Core.

It establishes the authoritative meanings of approved terms to preserve consistency, traceability, and shared understanding across all canonical artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

The Glossary defines:

* Canonical terminology.
* Approved term definitions.
* Terminology usage principles.
* Conformance requirements.

The Glossary does not define:

* Architectural decisions.
* Technical implementations.
* Project management.
* Runtime behavior.
* Organizational procedures.

---

# 3. Glossary Principles

The Glossary shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Unambiguous.
* Traceable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Canonical Terms

## Asset

A canonical unit of information managed within Kaivo.

---

## Identity

The canonical representation of a uniquely identifiable entity.

---

## Model

A conceptual structure that defines a domain and the relationships between its concepts.

---

## Specification

The normative requirements and constraints governing a particular type of artifact or instance.

---

## Template

A reusable structure that conforms to an associated specification.

---

## Registry

The authoritative collection of canonical instances of a defined artifact type.

---

## Map

A canonical representation of relationships among related entities or artifacts.

---

## Kit

A reusable collection of related Core artifacts providing a defined capability.

---

## System

A canonical organization of related Core Assets providing a defined capability.

---

## Canon

The authoritative collection of approved Kaivo artifacts.

---

## Governance

The principles, responsibilities, and authority governing the creation, approval, publication, and maintenance of the Kaivo Canon.

---

# 5. Terminology Requirements

Canonical terminology shall:

* Preserve approved meanings.
* Be used consistently across all artifacts.
* Remain uniquely identifiable.
* Avoid conflicting definitions.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Glossary does not:

* Redefine approved canonical concepts.
* Introduce conflicting terminology.
* Replace normative definitions established by authoritative artifacts.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every canonical term shall possess one authoritative meaning.

---

### C2

Canonical terminology shall remain consistent across the Kaivo Canon.

---

### C3

Approved terms shall not be redefined outside the approved governance process.

---

### C4

The Glossary shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C5

The Glossary shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

A document conforms to this Glossary if it:

* Uses approved canonical terminology.
* Preserves authoritative meanings.
* Maintains consistency with approved artifacts.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Documentation Standard.
