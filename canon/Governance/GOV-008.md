# 1. Purpose

The Approval Policy defines the canonical principles governing the approval of Kaivo Core artifacts.

It establishes the requirements that shall be satisfied before an artifact becomes an approved canonical artifact within Kaivo Core.

---

# 2. Scope

The Approval Policy defines:

* Approval principles.
* Approval requirements.
* Approval responsibilities.
* Approval boundaries.
* Conformance requirements.

The Approval Policy does not define:

* Architecture redesign.
* Project planning.
* Repository implementation.
* Deployment procedures.
* Runtime behavior.

---

# 3. Approval Principles

Artifact approval shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Verifiable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 4. Approval Requirements

Before approval, an artifact shall:

* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Preserve approved canonical terminology.
* Maintain consistency with approved artifacts.
* Respect established dependencies.

---

# 5. Governance Requirements

Approval shall:

* Follow the defined governance responsibilities.
* Respect the defined decision authority.
* Preserve approved canonical artifacts.
* Conform to the Document Lifecycle.
* Maintain artifact traceability.

---

# 6. Boundaries

The Approval Policy does not define:

* Executive prioritization.
* Architecture design.
* Technical implementation.
* Repository workflows.
* Runtime execution.

---

# 7. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Only artifacts satisfying all applicable canonical requirements shall be approved.

---

### C2

Approval shall preserve approved canonical terminology.

---

### C3

Approval shall maintain consistency across approved artifacts.

---

### C4

Approval shall follow the defined governance process.

---

### C5

Approval shall preserve artifact traceability.

---

### C6

The Approval Policy shall conform to the Core Ontology.

---

### C7

The Approval Policy shall conform to the Core Asset Model.

---

# 8. Conformance

A governance implementation conforms to this policy if it:

* Applies the defined approval requirements.
* Preserves approved canonical artifacts.
* Maintains consistency across the Kaivo Canon.
* Conforms to the Governance Charter.
* Conforms to the Decision Authority Model.
* Conforms to the Document Lifecycle.
