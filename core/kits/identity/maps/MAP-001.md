# 1. Purpose

The Identity Map provides the canonical representation of relationships among Identity instances within Kaivo Core.

It visualizes how identities are conceptually connected while preserving the semantic integrity of each Identity.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Map defines:

* Representation of Identity relationships.
* Map conformance requirements.
* Map responsibilities.
* Map boundaries.

The Identity Map does not define:

* Identity semantics.
* Identity creation.
* Identity validation.
* Authentication.
* Authorization.
* Storage mechanisms.
* Runtime behavior.
* User interface behavior.

---

# 3. Map Overview

The Identity Map provides a conceptual view of Identity relationships.

It represents relationships without modifying the Identity, structure, or semantics of any participating Identity instance.

---

# 4. Map Responsibilities

The Identity Map shall:

* Represent canonical relationships among Identity instances.
* Preserve the integrity of every Identity.
* Maintain semantic consistency.
* Remain compatible with all governing Core artifacts.

The Identity Map shall not redefine Identity or introduce additional semantics.

---

# 5. Relationship Requirements

Every relationship represented by the Identity Map shall:

* Connect valid Identity instances.
* Preserve the Identity of each participating instance.
* Remain semantically consistent.
* Conform to the Core Ontology.

---

# 6. Map Principles

The Identity Map shall be:

* Canonical.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Identity Map does not define:

* Organizational structures.
* Business workflows.
* Permission models.
* Runtime state.
* Application-specific behavior.
* Storage implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

The Identity Map shall represent relationships among valid Identity instances.

---

### C2

Relationships shall not modify the Identity of participating instances.

---

### C3

The Identity Map shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

### C4

The Identity Map shall conform to the Identity Model.

---

### C5

The Identity Map shall conform to the Identity Specification.

---

### C6

The Identity Map shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An implementation conforms to the Identity Map if it:

* Represents relationships among valid Identity instances.
* Preserves canonical Identity.
* Maintains semantic consistency.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Specification.
