# IDR-001 — Identity Registry

**Status:** Production Candidate
**Authority:** Kaivo Core
**Version:** 1.0

**Depends On:**

* CORE-000 — Core Ontology

* IDM-001 — Identity Model
* IDS-001 — Identity Specification

---

# 1. Purpose

The Identity Registry provides the canonical registry for Identity instances within Kaivo Core.

It establishes the authoritative collection of Identity instances while preserving the conceptual principles defined by the Identity Model and the structural requirements defined by the Identity Specification.

---

# 2. Scope

The Identity Registry defines:

* The authoritative collection of Identity instances.
* Registry conformance requirements.
* Registry responsibilities.
* Registry boundaries.

The Identity Registry does not define:

* Identity semantics.
* Identity creation.
* Authentication.
* Authorization.
* Storage mechanisms.
* Runtime behavior.
* User interface behavior.

---

# 3. Registry Overview

The Identity Registry serves as the authoritative registry of Identity instances.

Each registered Identity shall conform to the Identity Specification and remain uniquely referenceable.

---

# 4. Registry Responsibilities

The Identity Registry shall:

* Maintain authoritative records of Identity instances.
* Preserve unique referenceability.
* Preserve canonical Identity.
* Maintain compatibility with governing Core artifacts.

The Identity Registry shall not redefine or modify Identity.

---

# 5. Registry Requirements

Every Identity recorded in the registry shall:

* Conform to the Identity Model.
* Conform to the Identity Specification.
* Conform to the Core Asset Model.
* Conform to the Core Ontology.
* Represent exactly one identifiable entity.

---

# 6. Registry Principles

The Identity Registry shall be:

* Authoritative.
* Consistent.
* Traceable.
* Reusable.
* Stable.
* Implementation-neutral.

---

# 7. Boundaries

The Identity Registry does not define:

* Business workflows.
* Organizational structures.
* Permission models.
* Runtime state.
* Application-specific behavior.
* Storage implementation.

---

# 8. Canonical Constraints

### C1

Every registered Identity shall represent exactly one identifiable entity.

---

### C2

Every registered Identity shall possess exactly one canonical Identity.

---

### C3

The Identity Registry shall preserve the Identity of every registered instance.

---

### C4

The Identity Registry shall not redefine Identity.

---

### C5

Every registered Identity shall conform to the Identity Specification.

---

### C6

The Identity Registry shall remain implementation-neutral.

---

# 9. Conformance

An implementation conforms to this registry if it:

* Maintains authoritative Identity records.
* Preserves canonical Identity.
* Maintains unique referenceability.
* Conforms to the Core Ontology.
* Conforms to the Core Asset Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Model.
* Conforms to the Identity Specification.
